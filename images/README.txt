PUT YOUR PHOTOS HERE
====================
The site shows an icon until a photo with the right name exists, so you can add photos one at a time.
Use .jpg files, lowercase names, under 200 KB each (compress at squoosh.app).

images/hero.jpg                      Main photo on the home page (about 1200 x 900 px)
images/logo.png                      Your logo (then set logo: "images/logo.png" in js/main.js)

images/categories/mobility.jpg
images/categories/diagnostics.jpg
images/categories/respiratory.jpg
images/categories/wound-care.jpg
images/categories/furniture.jpg      (about 600 x 660 px)

images/products/1.jpg ... 12.jpg     One per product. The number is the "id" in js/products.js (about 800 x 600 px)

Only use photos you own, took yourself, or have permission or a licence to use.
Your suppliers can often give you product photos.
